Donor Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: Between 18 and 65 years.
  • Weight: Minimum 50 kg.
  • Health: Should be in good health, free from any major diseases.
  • Hemoglobin: Minimum 12.5 g/dL.
  • Donation Gap: At least 3 months since your last donation.
  • No Tattoos/Piercings: Within the last 6 months.

Post-Donation Guidelines

Do's:

  • Drink plenty of fluids for the next 24-48 hours.
  • Eat a healthy meal rich in iron.
  • Keep the bandage on for at least 4-5 hours.

Don'ts:

  • Avoid strenuous physical activity for the rest of the day.
  • Do not consume alcohol for at least 24 hours.
